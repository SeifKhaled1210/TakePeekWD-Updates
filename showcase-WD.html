Combat Features

Precision aiming, automated fire, and recoil control

Aimbot

Full-featured aim assist with FOV circle, adjustable smoothing, and bullet prediction for accurate engagements.

  • FOV circle overlay with adjustable radius
  • Smoothing slider (1 – 20) for natural movement
  • Bone selection: Head, Neck, Chest, Pelvis
  • Visibility check & team check filters
  • Target lock to stay on current target
  • Bullet prediction: drop + travel-time lead
  • Hold or Toggle activation mode
  • 8 configurable key binds
FOV Prediction Smoothing 8 Keys
Triggerbot

Automatic fire when crosshair is on target, with configurable delay for human-like timing.

  • Configurable delay: 0 – 200 ms
  • Custom key bind selection
  • Works alongside aimbot or standalone
Delay Key Bind
No Recoil / Sway / Spread WIP

Weapon stabilization features to eliminate recoil, sway, and bullet spread. Currently in development.

  • No Recoil: removes vertical/horizontal kick
  • No Sway: eliminates weapon idle sway
  • No Spread: tightens bullet grouping
